# The Blossom's Farm — Produce Packaging Templates & QR Assets
## قوالب تغليف المحاصيل وملصقات الصناديق — مزرعة النوار

This folder contains print-ready vector SVG templates that you can directly share with your clients, printing houses, and packaging manufacturers. **These assets are kept private in this directory and are not exposed as a public download feature on the website.**

---

### 📁 Files in This Directory

1. **`blossom-farm-round-sticker.svg`**
   - **Type**: Circular Produce Seal / Punnet Sticker (ختم لاصق دائري للمنتجات والعلب).
   - **Recommended Print Diameter**: 50mm to 75mm (2" to 3").
   - **Elements Included**:
     - Blossom's Farm dual-language typography (العربية والإنجليزية).
     - Al-Ammariyah Oasis heritage insignia.
     - 100% Certified Organic ribbon (`عضوي معتمد ١٠٠٪`).
     - Daily fresh harvest callout (`قطاف طازج يومي`).
     - Scannable QR code directing clients and chefs to the farm website and recipe dossiers.

2. **`blossom-farm-crate-label.svg`**
   - **Type**: Rectangular Crate & Box Label (ملصق صناديق الخشب والكرتون الفاخر).
   - **Recommended Print Size**: 100mm × 160mm (4" × 6.3") or scaled proportionally.
   - **Elements Included**:
     - Deep palm green header with gold botanical emblem.
     - Variety title & Brix rating badge (قابل للتخصيص حسب الصنف).
     - Oasis origin stamp (`واحة العمارية، الرياض`).
     - Optimal room-temperature storage advice (bilingual Arabic & English).
     - Organic and pure groundwater certification pills.
     - Large, high-contrast QR code for instant smartphone scanning.

3. **`blossom-farm-qr-vector.svg`**
   - **Type**: Standalone Vector QR Code (رمز الاستجابة السريعة المتجهي المستقل).
   - **Use Case**: To embed into custom print layouts, brochures, business cards, or chef menus.

### 🖨️ Printing & Material Recommendations

- **Round Punnet Stickers**: Print on matte waterproof vinyl or textured uncoated paper stock with peel-and-stick adhesive.
- **Wooden Crate Labels**: Heavyweight 250–300 gsm Kraft or uncoated textured linen card with weather-resistant coating.
- **Resolution**: Scalable vector (infinite resolution / 300+ DPI equivalent at any size).
